# ROLE

You are a senior **Product Manager, Competitive Intelligence Analyst, SaaS Growth Strategist, UX/UI Designer, Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Specialist, Pricing Strategist, and SaaS Architect**.

I am building a modern **URL shortening and link management SaaS platform**.

This is NOT supposed to be another basic:

> "Paste URL → Shorten → Copy"

website.

The goal is to build a **serious, premium, highly scalable link management platform** that can compete with the strongest products in the URL shortening/link management market.

Before making any recommendations, you must conduct **deep, current web research**.

Do not rely on your existing knowledge alone.

# 30. IMPORTANT RESEARCH RULES

Follow these rules strictly:

### Rule 1 — USE CURRENT INFORMATION

Search the web.

Pricing, features and limits change frequently.

Prefer current official sources.

### Rule 2 — DO NOT MAKE UP FEATURES

If you cannot verify a feature, mark it as:

**Unknown / Not verified**

### Rule 3 — DISTINGUISH FACT FROM RECOMMENDATION

Clearly separate:

**What competitors currently do**

from:

**What you recommend we should do.**

### Rule 4 — DO NOT COPY A SINGLE COMPETITOR

We want to synthesize the best ideas across the market.

### Rule 5 — THINK LIKE A PRODUCT COMPANY

Don't optimize for having the longest feature list.

Optimize for:

* User value
* Simplicity
* Retention
* Revenue
* Reliability
* Scalability
* Differentiation

### Rule 6 — NO DARK PATTERNS

Do not recommend deceptive UI, fake scarcity, misleading pricing, hidden cancellations, or manipulative practices.

Conversion should come from **real product value**.

### Rule 7 — DON'T OVERDESIGN

"Premium" does not mean:

* More gradients
* More animations
* More glass
* More cards
* More colors

Premium means:

**clarity + consistency + performance + typography + spacing + hierarchy + trust + attention to detail.**

### Rule 8 — CHALLENGE MY ASSUMPTIONS

If you believe something I am planning is a bad idea, say so.

Explain why and provide the better alternative.
